# Keel: Better Structures

**Vanilla Minecraft, with structures worth exploring, on the newest version.**

YUNG's structure overhauls turn mineshafts, strongholds, temples, fortresses and ocean monuments
into places you want to find. They usually reach a new Minecraft version weeks or months after
it comes out. This pack plays them now, through community ports, and switches each one to the
official release as soon as it exists.

## What is inside

- **Better structures:** YUNG's Better Mineshafts, Strongholds, Nether Fortresses, Ocean
  Monuments, Jungle Temples, Desert Temples, Witch Huts and End Island; YUNG's Bridges and
  Extras; Travelers' Titles. Dungeons and Taverns and Towns and Towers add more, in the same
  vanilla style.
- **Performance:** Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling, Dynamic FPS,
  ModernFix.
- **Quality of life:** Jade, AppleSkin, Mouse Tweaks, Xaero's Minimap and World Map, Mod Menu.
- **Modkeel Companion** (optional): if the game crashes, the next start names the likely cause
  and fixes it in one click. Worlds are backed up before the set of mods changes. It adds nothing
  to gameplay and sends nothing unless you choose to share a crash; turn it off in your launcher
  if you do not want it.

## How it is tested

Every version of the pack is started on a real server with all its mods, and every structure
the mods add is placed in the world (197 of 198 in this version; the Dungeons and Taverns bunker
only fits under high terrain, so the test cannot place it at a fixed spot). Then a real client
joins a world. A version that fails is not released. One mod stays out for now: YUNG's Better Dungeons, whose port failed to place
one of its dungeons.

## About the ports

Some mods in this pack are **community ports**: builds of public forks that update a mod to a
Minecraft version its author does not support yet. They are **not made or endorsed by the
original authors.**

- **Report problems to us**, on [GitHub](https://github.com/Modkeel/ports/issues), never to the
  original mod's author.
- Each port is replaced by the official build as soon as one is published for this version.
- The ports are open source (LGPL-3.0, like the originals). The jars not hosted on Modrinth are
  downloaded from GitHub: [Modkeel/ports](https://github.com/Modkeel/ports/releases) for our
  builds, with the exact source commit of each, and the ModernFix fork's own release.
